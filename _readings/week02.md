## Mandatory readings

Waldron, Jeremy. "The rule of law and the role of courts." *Global Constitutionalism* 10, no. 1 (2021): 91-105. <https://doi.org/10.1017/S2045381720000283>

Vanberg, Georg. "Constitutional Courts in Comparative Perspective: A Theoretical Assessment." *Annual Review of Political Science* 18, no. 1 (2015): 167-185. <https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-polisci-040113-161150>

Melton, James, and Tom Ginsburg. "Does De Jure Judicial Independence Really Matter?: A Reevaluation of Explanations for Judicial Independence." *Journal of Law and Courts* 2, no. 2 (2014): 187-217. <https://doi.org/10.1086/676999>
